The moments

When do you love the Flats most?

Your answer picks the room.

The grey day

Cloud lets the grass show its true colour. Warm, deep, held.

Try: Pampas Mural

The bright day

Sun washes the field pale and opens it up. Light, airy, easy.

Try: Merino pale, Gold Waves, Ochre Wall

The changing day

Sun and cloud take turns across the grass. Never the same twice.

Try: Merino Orange, or a limewash wall
