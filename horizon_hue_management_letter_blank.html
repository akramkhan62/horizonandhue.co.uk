Dear Building Manager,

I am writing to introduce Horizon & Hue, a specialist colour consultancy practice based in East London. I work with residential properties that face canals, parks, wetlands and other qualifying views — reading the colour language of the view outside the window and translating it into a coherent interior colour scheme for the home.

The properties in your building face one of the most distinctive views in East London. The canal, the warehouse facades, the industrial heritage of the surrounding landscape — these contain a specific colour palette that belongs to this particular part of the city. My methodology, called The Locale, reads that palette and brings it inside.

"Every home has a view. Every view has a colour language. Horizon & Hue reads it and brings it inside."

I am a Dulux Select accredited decorator with over 20 years of specialist finishing experience on period and contemporary properties across East and North London. I hold £2,000,000 public liability insurance through Simply Business.

My services begin with a free First Look — a 30 minute visit to a resident's home where I read the view, walk the room and give an initial colour direction. No obligation. No preparation required.
