The Locale Methodology

Horizon & Hue operates a proprietary colour methodology called The Locale — a view and vibe led approach that reads the geography outside a client's window and translates it into a room-by-room colour scheme. The methodology identifies five colour threads present across East London's multicultural landscape: Gold, Magenta, Green, Teal and Red-Earth.

"Every home has a view. Every view has a colour language. Horizon & Hue reads it and brings it inside."

Wallpaper and paint are specified as part of written Locale Briefs delivered to clients. Products are selected for their resonance with the Locale thread identified for each room — not by trend or generic taste.

The Six Locale Categories

Parks & Gardens · Water & Wetlands · Cemeteries & Woodland · Garden Squares · Streets & Markets · Maritime & Industrial

Each Locale generates a primary colour thread — Green, Teal, Deep Green, Gold, Magenta or Red-Earth — specified across three depths: Whisper, Dialogue and Declaration. Three atmospheres guide the tonal direction: Open Sky, Shelter and Horizon.
