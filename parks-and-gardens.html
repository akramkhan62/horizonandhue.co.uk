The reading
"Cypress and chinar. A garden that has always known how to organise itself."

The Chahar Bagh — the Persian four-part garden — is the structural origin of every Victorian park these homes face. Lloyd Park, Victoria Park, Clissold Park all carry that same geometry, laid out a century before anyone called it design.

What your window holds is filtered light through canopy, and a green that shifts by the hour without ever losing what it is. Green Thread leads here — the colour that was always going to answer this particular view.
