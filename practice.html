The Practice

Materials that bring the outside in.

I work with the materials that bring the outside to inside, where season acts as the accent and depth is set by the flow of the space. The colour is never chosen from a fan deck. It's read from what's already there.

Three readings, every property

One method. Three registers.

Every site I work on falls into one of three readings — not a style choice, a reading of what's genuinely there: a view, a waterway, or a street's own culture.

Parks & Gardens
Green Thread
Formal gardens, garden squares, open ground, woodland and cemetery edges. Enclosure to openness — one family of green, read differently by what surrounds it.
Canals & Maritime
Teal & Red-Earth Thread
Waterways, docks, warehouse conversions, the industrial heritage that still lines East London's canal corridors. Water above, brick below.
Markets & Worship Places
Gold & Magenta Thread
Street culture, market colour, the tilework of Brick Lane's Jamme Masjid. Culture and lifestyle read as directly as any view.
How it works, practically

Read. Specify. Accent.

01
The Reading
The view, the water, or the street sets the thread. Not a mood board — a specific colour lineage, traceable to what's actually outside.
02
The Depth
Set by the flow of the space, not personal taste alone. A hotel lobby in constant motion reads differently from a snug that holds people still. The room's own rhythm decides how loud the colour speaks.
03
The Accent
Season moves through the accent layer — a wall, a wallpaper, a mural — while the base scheme stays fixed. Ongoing, not one-and-done.
For developments & hospitality

A relationship, not a one-off.

Base schemes are specified once. Accents can be revisited each season — a genuine, low-cost reason to maintain a working relationship across a property's lifetime, not just at handover.

Enhancement
The base holds. The accent moves with the year. A standing arrangement, not a single commission.
