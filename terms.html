Sample cards

Price and ordering

A sample set is £15, including UK postage. It has three hand-painted cards for one season, one for each moment. Every paint is named on the card.

To order, message me on WhatsApp with your name and address. I'll reply with the price and my bank details. Your order is confirmed when your payment arrives. I post the cards within 5 working days of payment.

I will never ask for card details by message.

Colour

The cards are painted by hand with the paints named on them. Colour changes with light, so they show the paint, not a guarantee of how a whole room will look. Pictures on the website are illustrations.

Changing your mind

You can cancel within 14 days of receiving your cards, without giving a reason. Tell me by message, email or post. Then send the cards back within 14 days of telling me. Return postage is yours to pay. I refund the full £15 within 14 days of getting them back, or of you showing me proof you posted them.

If the cards arrive damaged or aren't as described, tell me and I'll replace them or refund you.

Credit

If you book Plan it or decorating work within 60 days of buying a sample set, the £15 comes off your bill.

Home visits and decorating work

Free home visit

A free 20-minute visit within 3 miles (5 km) of Plaistow, E13, and further out when my diary allows. There is no obligation to book anything.

Plan it and Have it done

Prices are confirmed in writing before any work starts. Materials are quoted separately. Special finishes, limewash and murals are quoted once I've seen the wall.

The Plan it fee is credited against decorating work booked within 60 days.

Your right to cancel

If you agree work with me in your home, or by message, phone or online, you have 14 days to cancel without giving a reason. I give you a cancellation notice and form in writing when you book.

If you want work to start within those 14 days, you need to ask me in writing. If you then cancel, you pay for the work done up to that point. Once the work is finished at your request, the right to cancel ends.

Paint, wallpaper or materials mixed or ordered specially for your home can't be returned once ordered, and I'll tell you before I order them.

Insurance

I hold public liability insurance. Any specialist I bring in holds their own.

Reviews and photos

Founding clients get the colour plan and sample cards free. In return, I ask for a photo of the finished room and a short review. I ask for an honest review, good or bad, and whenever I share it I say it came from a founding client.

I only photograph or publish your room with your written permission. I never show your house number, street or anything that identifies your home.

Your details

What I collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number and postcode, when you use the booking form or message me.
  • Your postal address, to post sample cards or visit you.
  • Photos of your room, if you send them.
  • Payment records, for my accounts.

Why

To reply to you, post your cards, carry out the work you've asked for, and keep the records the law requires. I don't sell your details or send marketing you haven't asked for.

Who else sees them

Only the services I use to run the business: Formspree (the booking form), WhatsApp, my bank (Starling), and Royal Mail for posting cards. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google.

How long I keep them

If you become a client, I keep your records for as long as tax law requires, usually six years. If you only enquire, I delete your details after 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details I hold about you. Message or email me. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
